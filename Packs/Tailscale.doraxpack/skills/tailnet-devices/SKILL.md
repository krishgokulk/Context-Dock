# Tailscale Workflow

1. Open **Tailscale Devices** from Global Context (`⌥⌥` / `⌘⌘`) or inside Tailscale.
2. Press `↵` to copy the peer's Tailscale IP, or `⌘K` to copy MagicDNS, copy `ssh <host>`, or open Tailscale Admin Console.
