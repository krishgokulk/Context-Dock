# Spotify Workflow

1. Open **Spotify Now Playing** or **Spotify Controls** from Global Context (`⌥⌥` / `⌘⌘`) or when Spotify is frontmost.
2. Press `↵` to toggle Play/Pause or open `⌘K` to skip tracks, copy the track title, or copy the `spotify:track:` URI.
