# Research Citation Extractor

When researching a web page in Safari:

1. **Read** the full page content via `safari.pageContent`.
2. **Identify** primary claims, statistics, and direct quotes.
3. **Cite** each finding with the exact page title and URL.
4. **Flag** unverified assertions or claims without sources.
5. **Format** output as a structured reference with markdown links.

Always distinguish between facts stated on the page and your own inferences.
