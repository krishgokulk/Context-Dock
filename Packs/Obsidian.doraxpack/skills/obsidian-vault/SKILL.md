# Obsidian Workflow

1. Open **Search Obsidian Notes** to browse recent notes with Markdown previews.
2. Press `↵` to jump straight into a note via `obsidian://open`, or `⌘K` to Reveal in Finder or copy a `[[Wikilink]]`.
3. Use **Quick Capture to Obsidian** to append a new Markdown note into any vault subfolder.
