# Apple Notes Workflow

1. Open **Search Apple Notes** from Global Context or when Apple Notes is active.
2. Press `↵` to jump straight to that note inside Apple Notes, or `⌘K` to copy its title or full body.
3. Run **Create Apple Note** to save a quick note straight into any folder.
